Bike Safari Destinations

From gentle countryside rides to challenging mountain trails, find the perfect bike safari for your adventure level.

Lake Bunyonyi Circuit

Scenic & Moderate

Cycle around one of Africa's most beautiful lakes, visiting islands, terraced hillsides, and traditional villages.

📏 40-60 km ⏱️ 1-2 Days 📈 Moderate

Ssezibwa Falls Route

Cultural & Easy

Easy cycling through rural communities to the sacred Ssezibwa Falls, combining culture with gentle exercise.

📏 25-35 km ⏱️ Half Day 📈 Easy

Mabira Forest Trails

Nature & Moderate

Mountain bike through Uganda's largest tropical rainforest with canopy walks and primate spotting opportunities.

📏 20-40 km ️ Full Day 📈 Moderate

Community-Based Tourism

Cycling for Good

Our bike safaris are designed to support local communities while providing you with an authentic cultural experience. We visit rural villages, meet local artisans, and contribute directly to community development projects.

Cycle through terraced farmlands, stop at local markets, share meals with village families, and learn about traditional ways of life. Your bike safari creates direct economic benefits for the communities you visit while offering a meaningful cultural exchange.

Safety First

Equipment & Safety

We provide top-quality equipment and experienced guides to ensure your bike safari is safe and enjoyable.

What We Provide

  • Quality mountain bikes or hybrid bikes
  • Safety helmets (mandatory)
  • Repair kits and spare tubes
  • Water bottles and hydration packs
  • First aid kits
  • Experienced cycling guides
  • Support vehicle for longer tours

🎒 What to Bring

  • Comfortable cycling clothes
  • Sturdy closed-toe shoes
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • Rain jacket (weather dependent)
  • Camera for photos
  • Personal medications
  • Energy snacks
