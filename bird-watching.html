Ornithology Tourism

A Birder's Paradise

East Africa, and particularly Uganda, is recognized as one of the world's premier bird watching tourism destinations. With over 1,060 recorded species in Uganda alone—more than any other African country—this region offers an unparalleled opportunity for both serious ornithologists and casual bird enthusiasts.

Our expert-led bird watching safaris take you through diverse habitats including tropical rainforests, papyrus swamps, savannah grasslands, and Albertine Rift valleys. Whether you're searching for the prehistoric Shoebill Stork, the stunning Great Blue Turaco, or rare Albertine Rift endemics, our knowledgeable guides ensure exceptional sightings.

From the misty forests of Bwindi to the wetlands of Mabamba Swamp and the savannahs of Queen Elizabeth National Park, every ecosystem offers unique avian treasures waiting to be discovered.

Target Species

Iconic Birds to Spot

East Africa's most sought-after bird species for serious birding tourism enthusiasts

🦤

Shoebill Stork

The prehistoric-looking giant with a massive bill, found in Uganda's papyrus swamps. A bucket-list bird for any serious birder.

🦅

African Fish Eagle

Africa's iconic raptor with a distinctive cry, commonly seen along rivers and lakes throughout the region.

🐦

Great Blue Turaco

A stunning, large turaco with brilliant blue and green plumage, often found in forest canopies.

🦜

Grey Parrot

Intelligent and vocal, these birds inhabit forest edges and are famous for their talking ability.

🦆

Papyrus Gonolek

A striking red and yellow bird endemic to papyrus swamps, best seen in Mabamba and other wetlands.

🦚

Rwandan Turaco

A rare, endemic species found in the highland forests of Rwanda and Uganda's western borders.

🦢

Saddle-billed Stork

Africa's largest stork with a distinctive yellow and black bill, often seen in wetlands.

🦉

Verreaux's Eagle-Owl

The world's largest owl, with impressive pink eyelids, found in rocky habitats and forests.

Prime Birding Sites

Best Bird Watching Locations

Expert-curated destinations offering the highest diversity and rarest species sightings

Mabamba Swamp

📍 Near Entebbe | Shoebill Capital

Uganda's premier Shoebill tracking destination. This expansive papyrus swamp on Lake Victoria offers the highest success rate for encountering this prehistoric giant.

  • Shoebill Stork (guaranteed sightings)
  • Papyrus Gonolek
  • Saddle-billed Stork
  • African Jacana
  • Over 300 wetland species

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

📍 Southwest Uganda | Albertine Rift Endemics

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Africa's richest forests for bird diversity, with numerous Albertine Rift endemic species.

  • African Green Broadbill
  • Blue-headed Sunbird
  • Shelley's Crimsonwing
  • Great Blue Turaco
  • Over 350 forest species

Queen Elizabeth NP

📍 Western Uganda | Savanna & Wetlands

Diverse habitats from savanna to wetlands make this park exceptional for bird watching tourism, with over 600 recorded species.

  • African Fish Eagle
  • Pied Kingfisher
  • Grey Crowned Crane
  • Open-billed Stork
  • Tree-climbing Lions area
