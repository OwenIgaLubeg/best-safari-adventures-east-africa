Our Story

Pioneering Authentic Luxury Safaris & Football Tourism.

Best Safari Adventures East-Africa was founded with a singular vision: to redefine luxury wildlife tourism in East Africa. Based in the heart of Kampala, Uganda, we recognized a growing demand for authentic, sustainable, and meticulously curated safari experiences that go beyond the ordinary.

Over the years, we have grown from a local tour operator into a premier, internationally recognized tourism brand. We specialize in crafting bespoke itineraries across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda, ensuring that every traveler experiences the raw beauty of Africa with uncompromising comfort and safety.

As a fully licensed and regulated tourism company, we work hand-in-hand with the Uganda Tourism Board and the Uganda Wildlife Authority to ensure that our operations not only delight our guests but also actively contribute to wildlife conservation and community empowerment.
