FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking your safari accommodation

Can I choose my own lodge, or do you assign it?

+
You have complete freedom! We provide a curated list of recommended lodges for each destination based on your budget and preferences. You can choose from our list, or if you have a specific lodge in mind, we will gladly check its availability and include it in your itinerary.

Are meals included in the accommodation price?

+
Most of our safari packages are offered on a "Full Board" basis, which includes breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Drinking water, soft drinks, and alcoholic beverages are typically excluded but can be added to your package upon request.

Is it safe to stay in tented camps in the wilderness?

+
Absolutely. Our recommended tented camps are highly secure. They are fenced or guarded 24/7 by trained security personnel. Staff will always escort you to and from your tent at night, ensuring your complete safety.

What is your cancellation policy for lodges?

+
Cancellation policies vary by lodge and season. Generally, cancellations made 30+ days before arrival incur no penalty. We will always clarify the specific cancellation terms of your chosen lodge before you confirm your booking.
