The Ultimate Alpine Adventure

Transformative Hiking Experiences in East Africa

Embarking on a mountain hiking tour in East Africa offers far more than a physical challenge; it is a transformative eco-tourism experience that connects you deeply with nature. From the misty, biodiversity-rich slopes of the Rwenzori Mountains to the snow-capped summit of Mount Kilimanjaro, our guided alpine trekking adventures provide unparalleled access to Africa's most spectacular landscapes.

Hiking tourism in Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda immerses you in pristine environments, rare alpine flora, and vibrant local mountain communities. Whether you are seeking safe mountain climbing expeditions, wildlife encounters along the trail, or the profound mental clarity that comes with summiting a peak, our expert-led itineraries ensure a sustainable, rewarding, and unforgettable journey to the roof of Africa.

The Big 6 Peaks

East Africa's Most Iconic Mountains

Discover the majestic peaks that define the East African landscape, each offering unique trails, ecosystems, and unforgettable summit experiences.

Mount Kilimanjaro

Tanzania

Africa's highest peak and the world's tallest free-standing mountain. Our guided Mount Kilimanjaro climbing tours offer multiple route options (Machame, Lemosho, Marangu) through diverse ecological zones, culminating in a breathtaking sunrise summit at Uhuru Peak.

5,895mElevation
5-9 DaysDuration

Mount Kenya

Kenya

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and Africa's second-highest mountain. Renowned for its rugged glacier-clad peaks and unique alpine ecosystems, Mount Kenya hiking expeditions provide a challenging yet highly rewarding trek through breathtaking Afro-alpine scenery.

5,199mElevation
4-6 DaysDuration

Mount Rwenzori

Uganda / DRC

Legend calls them the "Mountains of the Moon." This UNESCO-listed range offers some of the most spectacular and challenging alpine trekking in Africa. Hikers traverse misty rainforests, giant lobelias, and glaciated valleys to reach the snow-capped peaks of Margherita.

5,109mElevation
6-9 DaysDuration

Mount Elgon

Uganda / Kenya

An ancient, massive extinct volcano boasting one of the largest volcanic calderas in the world. Mount Elgon hiking tours are perfect for those seeking a less crowded, highly scenic trek featuring stunning waterfalls, hot springs, and diverse wildlife, including forest elephants.

4,321mElevation
4-5 DaysDuration

Mount Meru

Tanzania

Often considered the perfect acclimatization trek for Kilimanjaro, Mount Meru stands majestically within Arusha National Park. This mountain hiking adventure offers incredible wildlife viewing opportunities on the lower slopes, including giraffes, buffalo, and colobus monkeys.

4,562mElevation
3-4 DaysDuration

Mount Karisimbi

Rwanda / DRC

The highest of the eight volcanoes in the Virunga Mountains. A Mount Karisimbi hiking expedition is a strenuous but deeply rewarding two-day trek, often combined with gorilla trekking in Volcanoes National Park, offering panoramic views of the lush East African rift valley.

4,507mElevation
2 DaysDuration
