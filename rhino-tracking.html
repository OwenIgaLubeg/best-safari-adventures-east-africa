Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary: A Conservation Triumph

Located in Nakasongola District, Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary is the only place in Uganda where you can see wild white rhinos in their natural habitat. Established by Rhino Fund Uganda, this sanctuary plays a critical role in the conservation of endangered white rhinos in East Africa.

Your rhino tracking safari here is led by experienced, armed rangers who ensure both your safety and the well-being of the rhinos. Get within 20 meters of these gentle giants, learn about their behavior, and understand the vital conservation efforts keeping the species from extinction.

The Journey: Reintroducing Rhinos to National Parks

Discover the meticulous process of breeding, preparing, and transferring rhinos from Ziwa Sanctuary back to Uganda's historic national parks like Murchison Falls.

Phase 01

Breeding & Protection at Ziwa

Rhinos are bred in the secure, fenced environment of Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary. Here, they are protected from poachers and monitored 24/7 by dedicated rangers, allowing the population to grow safely.

Phase 02

Health & Behavioral Assessment

Veterinarians and conservationists conduct rigorous health checks. Rhinos are assessed for their readiness to adapt to a larger, wilder environment, ensuring they are strong and disease-free.

Phase 03

Park Security Preparation

Before any transfer, the destination park (e.g., Murchison Falls) undergoes massive security upgrades. Anti-poaching units are strengthened, and community engagement programs are launched to ensure local support.

Phase 04

The Great Translocation

Using specialized crates and heavy-lift helicopters or secure road transport, rhinos are carefully moved to their new home. The journey is monitored by vets to minimize stress.

Phase 05

Release & Monitoring in the Wild

Once released into the vast wilderness of the national park, the rhinos are fitted with tracking collars. Rangers monitor their movements and adaptation, marking a historic return to their ancestral lands.
