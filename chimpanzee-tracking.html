Meet Our Primate Cousins

Chimpanzees: 98% Human DNA

Chimpanzees are our closest living relatives, sharing 98.7% of our DNA. These highly intelligent primates live in complex social groups, use tools, and exhibit behaviors remarkably similar to humans. Observing them in the wild is a profoundly moving experience that connects us to our evolutionary heritage.

Uganda is home to an estimated 5,000 chimpanzees, making it one of the best destinations in Africa for chimpanzee tracking. Our expert primate guides lead you through dense rainforest trails, using their intimate knowledge of chimp behavior and vocalizations to locate these elusive apes.

Where to Track Chimpanzees in Uganda

Discover Uganda's premier chimpanzee tracking destinations, each offering unique primate experiences in stunning rainforest settings.

Kibale Forest National Park

Western Uganda

Uganda's primate capital with 1,500+ chimps. The most reliable chimpanzee tracking destination with 95% sighting success. Home to 13 primate species total.

Budongo Forest

Murchison Falls Area

Uganda's largest mahogany forest with 600+ chimps. Combine chimp tracking with Murchison Falls safari for the ultimate Uganda wildlife experience.

Kyambura Gorge

Queen Elizabeth NP

Track chimps in the stunning "Valley of Apes." This underground forest gorge offers dramatic scenery and the chance to combine primates with big game safaris.

The Trekking Experience

Into the Heart of the Rainforest

Your chimpanzee tracking adventure begins early morning with a briefing by experienced Uganda Wildlife Authority guides. You'll trek through dense tropical rainforest, following signs of chimp activity: feeding marks, nests, and vocalizations echoing through the canopy.

The trek typically lasts 2-5 hours depending on chimp location. Once located, you'll spend up to one hour observing these fascinating primates as they groom, play, feed, and interact. Witness mother-infant bonds, territorial displays, and the complex social dynamics that make chimpanzees so captivating.

Exclusive Experiences

Chimpanzee Habituation Experience

For the ultimate primate encounter, join researchers as they habituate wild chimpanzees to human presence.

Full-Day Habituation

Spend an entire day (6am-7pm) with researchers following chimpanzees from nest to nest. Witness the complete daily cycle of chimp life: waking, feeding, traveling, nesting. Limited to 4 visitors per day for an intimate, research-focused experience.

What You'll Experience

Observe unhabituated chimps displaying natural, wary behavior. Learn about primate research methods, conservation challenges, and the ongoing effort to protect Uganda's chimpanzee populations. Contribute directly to conservation through your participation fees.
